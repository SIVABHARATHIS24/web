Before you come

  1. Please come 10 minutes before your time for registration and vitals (BP, pulse, weight).
  2. Bring your old prescriptions, X-ray / MRI / CT films and reports, and the medicines you take now.
  3. For an injury, wear loose clothes. Do not remove a splint or plaster yourself.
  4. Your time is approximate: an emergency may cause a short delay. Please wait for your name to be called.
  5. If you cannot come, please call or WhatsApp the hospital so the time can be given to another patient.
