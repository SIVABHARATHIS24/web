Before you come

  1. Please arrive 15 minutes before your appointment time, so we can check your vitals (BP, pulse, weight) and do any tests needed.
  2. If you are not here at your appointment time, you will be seen as a walk-in patient in the regular OPD queue.
  3. Need an X-ray or blood test? You can come any time from 8 AM, so the reports are ready when the doctor sees you.
  4. Bring your old prescriptions, X-ray / MRI / CT films and reports, and the medicines you take now.
  5. For an injury, wear loose clothes. Do not remove a splint or plaster yourself.
  6. Your time is approximate: an emergency may cause a short delay. Please wait for your name to be called.
  7. If you cannot come, please call or WhatsApp the hospital so the time can be given to another patient.
