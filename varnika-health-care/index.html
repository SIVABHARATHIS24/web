Your visit

Simple, step by step

  1. 1

    Book or walk in

    Book a time online, or just come in. The doctor sees patients 5 PM – 9 PM, Monday to Saturday (not on Sundays).

  2. 2

    Consultation

    The doctor examines you and talks through your problem.

  3. 3

    Tests if needed

    Lab, X-ray and ECG 8 AM – 9 PM — come early for your tests and see the doctor with the reports ready.

  4. 4

    Treatment

    Medicines, plaster, splints or injections here. Surgery, only if needed, at a nearby hospital.

Timings

Not a 24-hour emergency service. For serious injuries outside these hours, go to the nearest hospital emergency department or call 108.
