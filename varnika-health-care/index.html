How your treatment works

Your visit, step by step

  1. Consultation. The doctor examines you and talks through your problem.
  2. Tests if needed. X-ray, ECG and lab tests are done at the clinic on the same visit.
  3. Treatment. Medicines, plaster, splints, injections or other procedures are done at the clinic.
  4. Surgery, only if needed. The doctor operates at a nearby hospital and continues your follow-up at the clinic.
