About the hospital

Varnika Speciality Hospital looks after injuries, fractures and bone, joint and spine problems for patients of all ages, from babies to older adults.

  • Doctor consultation
  • X-ray
  • ECG
  • Laboratory for basic investigations
  • Plaster, splints, injections and dressings
About surgeries: there is no operation theatre or in-patient ward here. When you need an operation, the doctor performs it at a nearby hospital and explains the arrangements beforehand.

What to bring

  • Earlier X-rays, scans and reports
  • Previous prescriptions and discharge summaries
  • The medicines you take now
  • Loose clothing, so the painful area is easy to examine

Outside opening hours

We are open 5 PM – 9 PM.

For serious injuries such as open fractures, heavy bleeding, head injury or road accidents, go straight to the nearest hospital emergency department or call 108 for an ambulance.
