Varnika Health Care began as a small family clinic with a big promise — to give every patient the care we'd want for our own family. Today we're a 120-bed multi-speciality hospital, and that promise hasn't changed.

Our mission

To deliver safe, affordable and compassionate healthcare to every person who walks through our doors — regardless of who they are.

Our vision

To be the most trusted hospital in our community, known for clinical excellence, honest advice and a truly human touch.

Our values

Compassion, integrity, patient safety, respect and continuous learning guide every decision we make.

Our story

From a neighbourhood clinic to a trusted hospital

What started with two doctors and a single consultation room has grown into a full-fledged hospital — built one patient, one relationship at a time.

  • 120 beds, including a 20-bed ICU and Level III NICU
  • 4 modular operation theatres and a 24/7 cath lab
  • In-house CT, MRI, digital X-ray and automated laboratory
  • Cashless treatment with leading insurers and TPAs
