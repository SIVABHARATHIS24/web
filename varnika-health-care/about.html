About the clinic

About the clinic

Varnika Speciality Hospital provides out-patient orthopaedic care. We look after injuries, fractures and bone, joint and spine problems for patients of all ages, from babies to older adults.

  • Doctor consultation
  • X-ray
  • ECG
  • Clinical laboratory for basic investigations
  • Plaster, splints, injections and dressings
About surgeries: the clinic has no operation theatre or in-patient beds. When you need an operation, the doctor performs it at a nearby hospital and explains the arrangements beforehand.
Before your visit

What to bring

  • Earlier X-rays, scans and reports
  • Previous prescriptions and discharge summaries
  • A list of the medicines you take now
  • Loose clothing that makes it easy to examine the painful area
Emergencies

Outside clinic hours

The clinic is open from 5 PM – 9 PM only. It is not a 24-hour emergency service.

For serious injuries such as open fractures, heavy bleeding, head injury or road accidents, go straight to the nearest hospital emergency department or call 108 for an ambulance.
